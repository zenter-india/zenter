Delete Your Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Zenter account and associated data. You do not need to be signed in to read these instructions.

Option 1 — From the mobile app

  1. Open the Zenter app and sign in.
  2. Tap the menu (☰) in the top-right corner, then tap Profile.
  3. Scroll down to the Privacy & account section.
  4. Tap Delete account, then confirm Delete permanently.

Option 2 — From the website

  1. Sign in at zenter.in/login.html.
  2. Go to your Profile page.
  3. Scroll down to the Privacy & account section.
  4. Click Delete account, then confirm Delete permanently.

If you're already signed in, you can jump straight to the confirmation dialog: zenter.in/profile.html#delete.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account is immediate and permanent. It removes:

  • Your profile (name, gender, exam centre, bio, travel/stay preferences)
  • All your connections and connection requests
  • All chat conversations and messages tied to those connections
  • Any contact-exchange history with other users

Feedback you've submitted and analytics events are retained but anonymized (no longer linked to your identity), consistent with our Privacy Policy's retention terms.

Need help?

If you're unable to sign in to delete your account yourself, email support@zenter.in from your registered contact and we'll process the deletion on your behalf.
